    The three tools on this chart

    ORB — Opening Range Breakout

    The high/low of the first minutes after the 9:30 AM ET equities open (shaded band). A candle closing above the range high signals buyers won the opening auction (long bias); a close below the low signals sellers (short bias). A poke beyond that closes back inside is a failed breakout — often a trap that fuels the other direction.

    FVG — Fair Value Gap

    A 3-candle imbalance: when candle 3's low is above candle 1's high (or the mirror on the way down), price moved so fast it left untraded prices — the shaded box. Price tends to revisit ("fill") these gaps; an unfilled gap below price acts like support (buyers left unfinished business), one above acts like resistance.

    Engulfing candles (the researched 3rd tool)

    Chosen over pin bars and momentum candles because it is the most objective one-bar shift signal: today's body completely covers the previous body in the opposite direction, meaning one side absorbed the other's entire move. With above-average volume it marks real participation, not noise. Shown as ▲/▼ markers.
